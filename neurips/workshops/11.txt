### Summary Sketch

1. **Overview about the paper**: The paper introduces GEAR, a framework designed to enhance the efficiency of key-value (KV) cache compression for large language models (LLMs) during generative inference. It addresses the challenges posed by high memory consumption and approximation errors in existing methods.

2. **Main contributions**: The main contributions include the development of GEAR, which integrates quantization with low-rank and sparse matrix techniques to achieve near-lossless performance at high compression ratios. The paper also presents empirical results demonstrating GEAR's superiority over existing methods.

3. **Proposed technique**: GEAR employs a three-component approach: (i) quantization of the majority of entries to ultra-low precision, (ii) low-rank matrix approximation to capture coherent quantization errors, and (iii) a sparse matrix to address individual outlier errors. This combination effectively reduces approximation errors while maintaining efficiency.

4. **Main results**: GEAR achieves significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency, outperforming state-of-the-art methods by up to 24.42% in accuracy at 2-bit compression and reducing peak memory usage by up to 2.39×, leading to throughput improvements of 2.1× to 5.07×.

5. **Applications or possible practical applications**: GEAR can be applied in scenarios requiring efficient inference of LLMs, such as real-time dialogue systems, content generation, and applications that demand high throughput and low memory usage.
