### Sketch of the Summary

1. **Overview about the paper**: The paper investigates self-improvement in Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning for Large Language Models (LLMs) without relying on supervised datasets. It introduces a novel reward function, Reasoning Advantage (RA), to enhance CoT reasoning capabilities using unstructured text data.

2. **Main contributions**: The paper presents a new reward function (RA) that effectively facilitates self-improvement in CoT reasoning. It also introduces the MMLU-FREE-FORM dataset for evaluating CoT reasoning in a free-form question-answering context.

3. **Proposed technique**: The authors propose using the RA reward function during the pretraining phase of LLMs to encourage the generation of meaningful CoT reasoning. The function is designed to differentiate between useful reasoning and random outputs, optimizing the learning process.

4. **Main results**: The RA function significantly improves zero-shot transfer accuracy on the GSM8K dataset by nearly 7% compared to other reward functions. The experiments demonstrate that RA is the only function that enables effective self-improvement of CoT reasoning on free-form QA data.

5. **Applications or possible practical applications**: The findings could be applied to enhance the reasoning capabilities of LLMs in various domains, particularly in scenarios where supervised datasets are scarce or unavailable. This could lead to more robust AI systems capable of complex problem-solving.
