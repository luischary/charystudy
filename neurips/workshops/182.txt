### Sketch of the Summary

1. **Overview about the paper**: The paper presents a theoretical framework for understanding action-conditional self-predictive reinforcement learning (RL) through the lens of the Bootstrap Your Own Latent (BYOL) framework. It aims to bridge the gap between theoretical insights and practical implementations in RL.

2. **Main contributions**: The authors unify the study of different BYOL objectives (BYOL-Π and BYOL-AC) and introduce a new variance-like objective (BYOL-VAR). They provide theoretical insights into how these objectives relate to low-rank approximations of dynamics and modified value functions.

3. **Proposed technique**: The paper analyzes the action-conditional BYOL objective (BYOL-AC) using an ordinary differential equation (ODE) framework. It establishes relationships between the learned representations of different objectives and their performance in RL tasks.

4. **Main results**: The empirical results show that BYOL-AC performs similarly to BYOL-Π across various tasks, with slight advantages in specific scenarios. The variance-like objective (BYOL-VAR) is shown to be less effective for direct RL applications.

5. **Applications or possible practical applications**: The findings can be applied to improve representation learning in RL, particularly in environments where action-conditional predictions are crucial. The insights could also inform future research on representation learning and action representation discovery.
