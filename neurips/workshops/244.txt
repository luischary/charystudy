### Summary Sketch

It introduces two main strategies: external search using Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) and internal search that distills search procedures directly into the LLM.

2. **Main contributions**: The authors contribute a multi-action-value (MAV) model capable of playing multiple board games at a high level, demonstrate the effectiveness of combining external and internal search methods, and provide empirical evidence of the model's performance against state-of-the-art bots, achieving Grandmaster-level play in chess.

3. **Proposed technique**: The MAV model integrates world modeling, value function, and policy function into a single Transformer architecture. It utilizes both external MCTS for planning and internal search techniques that allow the model to generate and evaluate potential future states in a single call.
