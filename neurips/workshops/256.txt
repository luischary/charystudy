### Summary Sketch

1. **Overview about the paper**: The paper presents a novel approach to aligning large language models (LLMs) with human preferences using a sample-efficient method based on contextual dueling bandits and Thompson sampling.
  
2. **Main contributions**: The authors introduce the SEA (Sample-Efficient Alignment) algorithm, which outperforms existing methods in terms of sample efficiency and alignment accuracy across various model scales and preference learning algorithms.

3. **Proposed technique**: The SEA algorithm integrates active exploration strategies into the alignment process, allowing for efficient online learning from human feedback. It employs Thompson sampling to select responses based on their expected rewards, optimizing the alignment process.

4. **Main results**: Extensive experiments demonstrate that SEA achieves significantly higher win rates and requires fewer queries compared to baseline methods, confirming its effectiveness in aligning LLMs with human preferences.

5. **Applications or possible practical applications**: The proposed method can be applied in various domains where LLMs are used, such as chatbots, content generation, and any application requiring alignment with user preferences.
