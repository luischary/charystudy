### Summary Sketch

1. **Overview about the paper**: The paper investigates the challenges of training recurrent neural networks (RNNs), particularly focusing on the issues of vanishing and exploding gradients. It introduces the concept of the "curse of memory," which complicates the learning process even when gradient issues are mitigated.

2. **Main contributions**: The authors provide a theoretical analysis of RNNs and deep state-space models (SSMs), revealing that the sensitivity of RNNs to parameter changes increases with memory length. They propose techniques to mitigate these issues, including normalization and reparametrization strategies.

3. **Proposed technique**: The paper discusses the use of diagonal connectivity in RNNs and SSMs to control the sensitivity of the hidden states to parameter changes. It also emphasizes the importance of careful parameterization to maintain stable learning dynamics.

4. **Main results**: The authors demonstrate that deep SSMs and gated RNNs (like LSTMs) perform better than traditional RNNs due to their architectural designs that help mitigate the curse of memory. Empirical results show that these architectures can effectively learn long-term dependencies.
