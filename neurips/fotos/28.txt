### Sketch of the Summary

It establishes that generating new strings from a language is fundamentally different from identifying that language from samples.

3. **Proposed technique**: The authors introduce a method that maintains a sequence of provisional languages consistent with observed samples and refines these as new strings are presented. The algorithm generates new strings from these provisional languages, ensuring they belong to the target language.

4. **Main results**: The paper proves that for any countable collection of languages, there exists an algorithm that can generate from the true language in the limit.
