    "summary": "The paper presents Stylus, a novel system for automatic adapter selection and composition in diffusion models, specifically targeting the generation of high-fidelity images. Stylus addresses the challenge of efficiently matching user prompts with relevant adapters from a vast database of over 100,000 adapters, many of which lack clear descriptions. The proposed method consists of a three-stage framework: Refine, Retrieve, and Compose. The Refiner generates improved descriptions and embeddings for adapters, the Retriever identifies relevant adapters based on the user's prompt, and the Composer segments the prompt into tasks and assigns the appropriate adapters. The evaluation of Stylus demonstrates significant improvements in visual fidelity, textual alignment, and image diversity compared to existing models, achieving better performance metrics and user preferences. Stylus also introduces StylusDocs, a curated dataset of 75,000 adapters with pre-computed embeddings, enhancing the evaluation process.",
