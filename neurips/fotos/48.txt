### Summary Sketch

1. **Overview about the paper**: The paper investigates the faithfulness of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning in large language models (LLMs) and evaluates the validity of a proposed metric for measuring this faithfulness across different model families.
  
2. **Main contributions**: The authors replicate and extend previous findings on CoT faithfulness, introduce a normalized metric to account for model biases, and challenge the interpretation of faithfulness as it relates to model accuracy.

3. **Proposed technique**: The paper employs a normalized version of the unfaithfulness metric that adjusts for a model's tendency to favor certain answer choices, allowing for a more accurate assessment of CoT faithfulness.

4. **Main results**: The study finds that while some models exhibit a V-shaped trend in unfaithfulness related to model size, the normalized metric reveals that smaller models may appear more faithful due to their biases. A strong correlation between normalized unfaithfulness and accuracy is also established.
