### Summary Sketch

1. **Overview about the paper**: The paper addresses the challenge of detecting out-of-distribution (OOD) inputs in machine learning systems, emphasizing the need for a broader approach to OOD detection that goes beyond just novel classes. It introduces a new benchmark called BROAD, which categorizes various types of distribution shifts and evaluates existing detection methods against them.

2. **Main contributions**: The paper's main contributions include the introduction of the BROAD benchmark, a comprehensive evaluation of existing OOD detection methods across multiple distribution shifts, and the development of a generative ensemble method that improves detection performance.

3. **Proposed technique**: The authors propose a Gaussian mixture model (GMM) based ensemble method that combines detection scores from various existing methods to enhance the robustness and consistency of OOD detection across different types of distribution shifts.

4. **Main results**: The results show that existing OOD detection methods perform well on novel classes but struggle with other types of distribution shifts. The proposed ensemble method outperforms all baseline methods in average detection performance across the different types of shifts.

5. **Applications or possible practical applications**: The findings have practical implications for deploying machine learning systems in real-world scenarios, particularly in safety-critical applications where detecting unexpected inputs is crucial.
