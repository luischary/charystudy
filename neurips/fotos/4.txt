### Sketch of the Summary

1. **Overview about the paper**: The paper addresses the issue of group robustness in machine learning models, particularly in the presence of spurious correlations. It introduces a novel two-stage training approach called JTT (Just Train Twice) that improves worst-group accuracy without requiring extensive group annotations during training.

2. **Main contributions**: The main contributions include the introduction of the JTT method, which effectively enhances worst-group performance while only needing group annotations for hyperparameter tuning on a small validation set. The paper demonstrates that JTT can close the performance gap between standard empirical risk minimization (ERM) and group distributionally robust optimization (group DRO).

3. **Proposed technique**: JTT consists of two stages: first, a standard ERM model is trained to identify misclassified examples; second, a final model is trained by upweighting these misclassified examples. This approach allows the model to focus on challenging groups that are often misclassified due to spurious correlations.

4. **Main results**: The results show that JTT significantly improves worst-group accuracy across four datasets (Waterbirds, CelebA, MultiNLI, and CivilComments-WILDS) compared to ERM and other methods that do not use training group information. JTT achieves an average worst-group accuracy improvement of 16.2% while maintaining competitive average accuracy.

5. **Applications or possible practical applications**: The JTT method can be applied in various domains where fairness and robustness are critical, such as natural language processing, image classification, and any scenario where models may be biased due to spurious correlations in the training data.
